The Kitchen

We don't translate Nigerian food for anyone. We just cook it with everything French technique has to offer.

Every ingredient on the tasting menu is sourced within a day's drive of Benin City — periwinkle from the creeks, suya spice ground in-house, palm oil we press ourselves. What changes is the hand: sous-vide, veloutés, clarified stocks, the discipline of a French pass applied to dishes your grandmother would recognise.

Fourteen seats. One sitting. No à la carte.

Chef Aiyekóóto Munson Chef-Patron
